Turn Over A New Leaf, Make A Leaf!

Build the leaf of your dreams, then watch nature try its very best to ruin it! Pick a tree, give your leaf a color, a personality and a name, and spend your hard-earned sap on tiny hats, bug buddies and questionable training programs. Then hang it on the branch and hope it survives whatever tornado, wildfire, flood, blizzard, hailstorm or earthquake comes next. Watch out for the LARGE ones.

Plays right in your browser, no download needed!

Plays entirely with the mouse. Press M to mute.
